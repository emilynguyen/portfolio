Website redesign @ HoneyBook

Led design for the refresh of HoneyBook’s marketing site following a major rebrand, an initiative focused on improving structure, brand messaging, and product comprehension.

‍

This involved collaborating with the design agency Study Hall on concept development, designing and producing visual assets for 40+ pages, and working with cross-functional and external partners on strategy, content, and Webflow development.

2 months post-launch, the site saw an 18% lift in page engagement and positive trends in trial conversions.

In addition to designing pages, I built a Figma web component library to support the redesign and ensure consistency and scalability. The library includes 1600+ assets, ranging from foundational atomic elements to complex block-level layouts.

‍

I worked closely with our development partners to align this with our Webflow component library, enabling us to build pages quickly and reliably.
